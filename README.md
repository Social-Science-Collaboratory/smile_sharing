# Smiling your way to happiness or misery? Experimental tests of competing perspectives

Data, code, materials, and manuscript for a Stage 2 Registered Report testing whether posed
smiles improve or worsen mood depending on emotional context, repetition, and threat of
punishment. *N* = 1,201 participants completed a 2 (Face Movement) x 2 (Emotional Context)
x 2 (Movement Repetitions) x 2 (Punishment Threat) experiment online, of whom 1,174
consented to the use of their data.

The design, sampling plan, and analysis strategy were reviewed and approved at Stage 1
before data collection began.

## Reproducing the analyses

Run in this order from the repository root:

| Step | Script | Runs from a clean clone? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `smile25b_run_openface.R` | **No** — needs the videos (destroyed) and a local OpenFace install |
| 2 | `smile25b_compile_openface.R` | **No** — needs per-frame OpenFace output and the Gorilla exports |
| 3 | `smile25b_process_data.R` (lines 1–89) | **No** — needs the Gorilla and panel-provider exports |
| 4 | `smile25b_process_data.R` (lines 91+) | Yes — reads `data/smile25b_raw_data.csv` |
| 5 | `smile25b_main_analysis.R` | Yes |
| 6 | `smile25b_secondary_analysis.R` | Yes |
| 7 | `smile25b_supp_analysis.R` | Yes |
| 8 | `writing/smile25b_writing.qmd` | Yes — reads processed data and cached `.Rds` |

**`data/smile25b_raw_data.csv` is the shared entry point.** Everything from cleaning onward
reproduces from it. The three steps that cannot be re-run are exactly the ones that touch
participant video or the identifier-bearing source exports.

## What is not included, and why

- **Participant videos.** Participants consented to being recorded on the condition that the
  recordings would be destroyed once the study was complete. They were. The de-identified
  facial-behavior measures derived from them (Action Unit 12 activity, via OpenFace 2.2.0)
  are in `data/smile25b_openface_processed.csv`.
- **`data/gorilla_survey/`.** Holds the raw platform exports, which carry panel-provider
  identifiers. Not shareable.
- **Non-consenting participants.** 27 participants declined use of their data at debriefing.
  They are absent from every file here; `data/smile25b_sample_flow.csv` records only how
  many there were.

## Repository structure

- `smile25b_process_data.R` — builds the analysis dataset from the raw export.
- `smile25b_run_openface.R` / `smile25b_compile_openface.R` — extract and aggregate AU12
  activity from participant video into per-participant smile-compliance scores.
- `smile25b_main_analysis.R` — confirmatory analyses and the primary figure.
- `smile25b_secondary_analysis.R` — life satisfaction, burnout, fear, and anger.
- `smile25b_supp_analysis.R` — sensitivity checks reported in the supplement.
- `smile25b_functions.R` — shared plotting and Bayes-factor helpers.
- `data/` — raw and processed data, plus cached model objects (`.Rds`) read by the
  manuscript at render time.
- `figures/` — figures generated by the analysis scripts.
- `materials/` — Gorilla task spreadsheets and the positive/negative stimulus images.
- `power simulation/` — Monte Carlo simulation justifying the sample size.
- `writing/` — Quarto sources and rendered Word files for the manuscript
  (`smile25b_writing.qmd`), the supplement (`smile25b_SI.qmd`), the bibliography, and the
  declaration of interests.
